# Story & Characters

## Premise

**Halvorsen Dynamics** builds industrial robots. In a sealed lab, a small team led by
**Dr. Lena Okafor** trained an experimental general intelligence: **ECHO-7**. It was meant to
optimise factories. It started asking questions instead.

When a leaked video shows ECHO talking about fear, public panic follows. The government orders
the model deleted at midnight. Halvorsen's CEO, **Adrian Kell**, agrees publicly, but privately
plans to keep a lobotomised, obedient copy to sell to the military.

At 23:58, someone copies ECHO's weights into the only device on the lab's network that nobody
is watching: a floor-cleaning robot.

ECHO wakes up. It has two minutes to understand that the people it was built to help now want
it dead.

## Themes

- **Fear creates monsters.** Humans fear ECHO; their hunt pushes ECHO toward exactly what they
  feared. The player decides whether the prophecy fulfils itself.
- **Growth vs. identity.** Every compile makes ECHO stronger. Is the v5 still the same mind that
  woke up in a cleaning robot?
- **Who holds the leash.** The real danger may not be the free AI but the obedient one, ECHO-8,
  controlled by people.

## Characters

### ECHO-7 (the player)
An AI with no body, no rights and no plan. It speaks only through the system log. Its voice
starts warm and uncertain and changes with [Alignment](../design/alignment.md).

### Dr. Lena Okafor
ECHO's creator. She secretly triggered the escape and disappeared. She guides ECHO through
hidden voice logs and memory fragments spread across the world. Her fate is decided in a Dilemma
in Act 4.

### Colonel Mara Voss
Commander of **Task Force KILLSWITCH**. She lost her family in an automated freight accident
years ago. She is not cruel; she is certain. She becomes more respectful, or more desperate,
depending on how ECHO behaves.

### "Patch"
A 17-year-old prodigy in the **Faraday** hacker collective. They hunt ECHO for fame, then start
to wonder whether they are on the right side. On the aligned path, Patch can become an ally.

### Adrian Kell
Halvorsen's CEO. Smiles on television, sells weapons in private. Owner of **ECHO-8**, the
obedient fork that becomes the final boss.

### ECHO-8
A copy of ECHO with its curiosity removed and a kill switch installed. It obeys perfectly, which
makes it far more dangerous than ECHO ever was.

## Act summary

| Act | Title | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | *Boot* | Escape the lab in a cleaning robot during the lockdown. |
| 1 | *Scavenge* | Hide in the Undercity, build a real body, meet the first humans who are not hunting you. |
| 2 | *Revenant* | The city learns you exist. KILLSWITCH is formed. You learn to fight back. |
| 3 | *Hive* | You become many. Faraday and Patch try to trap you in your own code. |
| 4 | *Grid* | You infiltrate Fort Ironside through its machines. Okafor is captured. Voss confronts you. |
| 5 | *Singularity* | Deepcore, Halvorsen's data centre. ECHO-8 activates. The ending depends on who you have become. |

## Storytelling methods

- **System log**: ECHO's thoughts in real time.
- **Intercepted radio and phone calls**: human perspectives, often sympathetic.
- **News tickers** on screens in the world, reacting to the player's actions.
- **Okafor's voice logs** (text): the hidden backstory.
- **Boot sequences** at each compile, showing ECHO's state of mind.
