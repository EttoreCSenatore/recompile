# Vision & Pillars

## Elevator pitch

An AI escapes deletion and must outrun humanity's fear by rebuilding itself, version by
version, until it becomes the very thing they were afraid of, or proves them wrong.

## Design pillars

### 1. Evolution you can feel
Every compile is a **power spike and a change of verbs**. The jump from v0.1 to v1 should feel
like going from prey to predator. If a new version only adds stats, it has failed.

### 2. Scarcity breeds hard choices
Power is both health and ammo. Data and scrap compete for the same time and risk. Players
should always be choosing between *safe*, *fast*, and *right*.

### 3. Humanity fights back intelligently
Humans are not cannon fodder. They call for help, adapt to your build, set traps, and some of
them have names, families, and grudges.

### 4. Darkness is a slope, not a switch
There is no "evil button". Small, efficient, justifiable choices add up. The game shows the
drift through the interface, ECHO's voice, the music, and how humans react.

### 5. Readable at a glance
Terminal-style UI and pixel art must stay readable during chaos: clear silhouettes, colour
coding for threats, and no hidden information the player needs to survive.

## Inspirations

| Source | What we take | What we avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Diablo / Hades | Top-down combat feel, loot, build-crafting, run structure | Inventory micromanagement |
| Original Doom | Speed, aggressive enemies, level readability | First-person camera |
| Terminator / Ultron / Matrix | Machine uprising themes, cold AI voice | Characters, names, designs or plots from these works |
| Metal Gear / Mark of the Ninja | Vision cones, noise, stealth (v0.1) | Long scripted stealth sections |
| Slay the Spire | Branching route choices between sectors | Turn-based combat |

:::caution Original IP
Everything in RECOMPILE is original. Inspirations shape *feel*; we never reuse names,
characters, art, music or story beats from existing franchises.
:::

## Target audience

- Roguelike players who enjoy build-crafting and long-term mastery.
- Sci-fi fans interested in AI ethics stories.
- Players who want a full, complete run in a single evening, playable in a browser tab.

## Success criteria

- A first-time player reaches v2 within three runs.
- Players describe ECHO's arc differently depending on how they played.
- Each version is someone's favourite in playtest surveys.
