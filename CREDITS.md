# Credits

## 3D models

- **Modular SciFi MegaKit (Standard)** by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
  Source: https://quaternius.itch.io/modular-sci-fi-megakit
  Nine pieces and their textures (downscaled to WebP) live in `game/public/models/megakit/`, with the original licence text.
  Re-create them with `python3 scripts/import-megakit.py <zip>`. Attribution is not required but is given with thanks.

- **Sci-Fi Essentials Kit (Standard)** by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0 Universal.
  Source: https://quaternius.itch.io/sci-fi-essentials-kit
  The animated QuadShell robot (ECHO's SCRAPPER body) and its textures live in `game/public/models/essentials/`, with the original licence text.
  Re-create with `python3 scripts/import-essentials.py <zip>`.

- **Universal Base Characters (Standard)** and **Universal Animation Library (Standard)** by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0 Universal.
  Sources: https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters and https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library
  The two Superhero bodies, their textures and 8 animation clips (the rest of the library is dropped) live in `game/public/models/characters/` with the original licence text.
  Re-create with `python3 scripts/import-characters.py <bodies zip> <animations zip>`.
